MIR — Modded Item Randomizer, v1.1.1 — loot sources.

**New in v1.1**
- **A new Loot Source Settings tab.** Every kind of container has its own section — chests, corpses, barrels and crates, vases / urns / jars / amphorae, wardrobes, and bookshelves — each with its own on/off switch, including a new **Spawn in chests** switch.
- **Vases, urns, jars and amphorae are their own loot source,** separate from barrels and crates, and off by default.
- **Tick what you want.** Barrels and vases each offer rings, potions, arrows, alchemy ingredients, or everything. Wardrobes offer cosmetic clothes, clothing and armour with stats, or everything. **Nothing ticked means nothing.**
- **Spawn chance and rolls per loot source.** Tick *Use its own chance and rolls* on any source to give it a different rate. Sources left unticked use the General tab's values.
- **A chance or roll count of 0 no longer finishes containers for good.**
- **Upgrading from v1.0.1?** Vases start switched off, and wardrobes with all three boxes unticked now give nothing. See `docs/CHANGELOG.md`, "What changes for an existing save".

**Requires** BG3 Script Extender (v29+) and Mod Configuration Menu (Nexus 9162), loaded above MIR. MIR disables itself if MCM is absent.
